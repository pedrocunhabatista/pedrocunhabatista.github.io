My experience across Europe, Africa and South America has taught me to look at sporting ambition alongside the organisation needed to support it. Working in Portugal, Nigeria, Egypt and Brazil has meant adapting to different resources, cultures and expectations.

I am interested in clubs entering a new chapter: professionalising their operations, restructuring their football department or defining a direction under new ownership. That includes SAFs in Brazil and groups working across more than one club.

01 / Sporting CP · 2013–2018

Learning the institution from the inside.

Sporting gave me a foundation across the functions that hold a football club together. As Assistant to the CEO, I supported academy restructuring across technical, medical, recruitment, logistics and support areas, and helped implement Sporting ISM to connect information across departments.

My responsibilities also included UEFA match operations liaison in the Champions League and Europa League, and serving as Executive Director of the Sporting Social Foundation. It was an education in the relationship between sporting performance, institutional responsibility and community.

  • Sporting Social Foundation: helped its development into a formal, financially sustainable institution recognised by government and EU bodies.

02 / Kebbi United FC · 2021–2023

Building around the reality of the club.

In Nigeria, I directed football operations at Kebbi United, a professional club in the north of the country, and managed two academies: one in northern Nigeria and one in southern Nigeria. My responsibilities covered club operations, scouting, coaching methodology and player development.

It brought a different responsibility: translating football ideas into structures that made sense locally. Player development had to be considered alongside the people, resources and community around it.

  • Player pathways: built development routes and technical structures linking young talent with the ambitions of the club.
  • Scouting and relationships: helped organise talent identification and strengthened football networks and stakeholder relationships across Nigeria.
  • Leadership in context: connected club decisions with grassroots development, education and community engagement.

03 / ENPPI West Football Academy · 2023–2024

Giving development a shared direction.

As Technical Director in Egypt, I worked on methodology, the training model and long-term player development. This included standardising coaching structures and talent evaluation so that daily training could serve a coherent development pathway.

The experience sharpened a question that still guides my work: how can a club provide continuity while leaving room for the individual qualities of its players?

  • Coaching processes: established a more consistent academy structure and approach to daily training.
  • Talent evaluation: improved assessment frameworks and long-term development planning, supporting alignment with international high-performance standards.
  • Football identity: connected academy development with clear playing and player-development principles.

04 / MyGamePlan · Global Operations · 2024–Present

Working with clubs.
Learning how they decide.

MyGamePlan has brought my work into direct contact with leading and top-division clubs across Europe, the United States, South America and Asia.

I help clubs connect recruitment, scouting, performance analysis and individual player selection with their football identity. The work includes positional profiles, squad planning, individual development plans and the use of data within a club’s own methodology.

I also learn from the people doing this work: how they define a need, challenge an assessment and arrive at a decision. Seeing these processes across different clubs deepens the perspective I bring to football leadership.

Brazil / Global · Helped establish MyGamePlan’s first club relationships in Brazil, Ecuador, the USA and Saudi Arabia.

Football knowledge across markets

From the game model to the squad.

Based in Brazil, I have deepened my knowledge of the South American football market while working with football directors, chief scouts, coaches and analysts internationally. The aim is to make analysis useful to the people responsible for the next decision.

This means linking game-model definition, positional requirements, recruitment and long-term squad planning. MyGamePlan provides a practical setting in which football data, coaching knowledge and executive decisions can be considered together.

05 / MindStats · Portugal / Global · 2025–Present

Understanding the mental dimension
of football performance.

CEO & Co-Founder

I co-founded MindStats with Carlos Martins. Together, we are developing an AI platform focused on the mental and behavioural dimension of football performance: how players respond to pressure, adversity and the demands of competition.

Our work connects player development, recruitment and the way clubs use information. Our aim is to bring performance data and mental-performance insight together, helping football professionals better understand a player’s development and suitability for a team.

From information to football questions.

  • Pressure and adversity: explore responses to demanding moments, mistakes and changes in the game.
  • Player development: connect behavioural and performance signals with individual development discussions.
  • Recruitment and team context: help clubs ask more informed questions about decision-making, consistency and competitive demands.

Executive vision

From identity to action.

The ability to build. The knowledge to execute. The leadership to integrate.

I believe sustainable success begins with a clear club identity: an honest understanding of what the institution represents, how it wants to compete and the value it wants to create for players, supporters and its community.

My experience allows me to build and implement the structures behind that ambition, from the game model and positional profiles to individual development, academy pathways, recruitment, analysis and technology. My greatest value, however, is connecting those areas through leadership, clear accountability and a shared direction.

A strong football organisation does not depend on one coach, one transfer window or one season. It creates continuity without becoming rigid, gives specialists the conditions to excel and turns knowledge into better decisions across the whole club.

Club identity→Game model→Profiles→Development→Recruitment→Performance
01

Identity & Direction

Define who the club is, what it stands for and where it is going. Identity should guide sporting decisions and give supporters a team they can understand and recognise.

02

Integrated Leadership

Connect the first team, academy, scouting, performance, medical, operations and management around shared priorities, clear responsibilities and open communication.

03

A Club Game Model

Translate identity, competitive objectives and league context into a flexible football framework that gives direction beyond any individual coach.

04

Profiles & Recruitment

Turn the game model into positional requirements. Recruit players and coaches for their fit, contribution and potential, combining observation, context and evidence.

05

Individual Development

Give every player a clear development plan with relevant objectives, regular feedback and shared ownership, from academy prospects to first-team professionals.

06

Academy Pathways

Treat youth development as a strategic pillar of the club. Align principles, profiles and transition plans so talent can progress naturally towards professional football.

07

Evidence & Technology

Use data and technology to make the club's own principles measurable, improve understanding, challenge assumptions and support faster, better-informed decisions.

08

Sustainability & Connection

Link sporting performance to financial discipline, asset development and social relevance, strengthening the relationship between the club, its supporters and its community.

My role is to set the direction, build where needed, understand the detail and lead the people who turn strategy into daily performance.

Direction to align. Knowledge to execute. Leadership to progress.
